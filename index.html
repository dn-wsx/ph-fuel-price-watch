What is driving it

Published estimates

The Facebook oil-price pages and the news carry industry estimates built on the real Singapore prices, which arrive a day before the free Singapore data does. To add one, open Actions → Update forecast and deploy → Run workflow and fill in the Published estimate fields.

Forecast vs. actual

All figures are pesos per liter. Forecast: our estimate. Actual: what the DOE announced (green rollback, red hike). Miss = forecast − actual: within ₱1₱1 to ₱2.50over ₱2.50 A plus means the forecast was too high (too little rollback or too big a hike).

* Rebuilt: what the model says from that week's finished Singapore prices, not a forecast made at the time. Actual adjustments come from the DOE's weekly Oil Monitor.

This is an estimate from public market data, not an official figure. Pump prices follow Singapore (MOPS) product prices, which are not free to access, so this site reads the daily Singapore prices that Korea's Opinet publishes a day later, corrects for them as actual adjustments are recorded, and blends in published industry estimates when it has them. Always check announcements from the DOE and your oil company.
